Why did my trade route deliver an unassigned good?

Traders load goods listed on the route first. If they have room left, they may carry other goods to balance stock between regions.

Is there already a trade route from the source region to the destination?

Yes, and this good is unassigned

Spare cargo space can explain the delivery. Check whether the source region had a surplus of this good and whether the trader had room left after loading the listed goods.

Example. The route from A to B lists grain. B also receives cabbage. If A has surplus cabbage and there’s room after loading the grain, the trader may take cabbage too.

No route, or I’m unsure

Open the trade panel and check whether the route list includes the correct source, destination, and direction. If you find that route, check the good against its list.

If there’s no route, the developer’s reply leaves that delivery unexplained.
